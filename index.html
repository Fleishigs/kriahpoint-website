A familiar classroom routine

Choose. Listen. Mark. Save.

Keep your attention on the student. The app gives each step a clear place.

01

Choose the student and sheet

Open the class list, choose a name, and select the reading sheet. Check the summary, then begin.

02

Listen and tap

Start the side timer. Tap a letter or nekuda to mark a mistake. Tap again to clear it. Use Undo whenever needed.

03

Mark the minute

The reading page turns amber at 0:55 and mint at 1:00. Tap Mark 1-minute word, then the word reached.

04

Finish and save

Check the summary and tap Save test. A clear confirmation tells you the test is safely stored on the device.

Your first test

At one minute, what do I do?

1. Look for the mint page and “One minute reached.”

2. Tap Mark 1-minute word.

3. Tap the word the student reached at the minute.

The marker records exactly 60 seconds, even if it takes a moment to select the word.

A few useful answers

Simple from the first test.

Does the Android app work without Wi-Fi?

After a device has connected and downloaded its school catalog, reading tests can be saved locally without Wi-Fi. Saved work is kept until the server acknowledges a successful upload.

How do I connect a new device?

Your school administrator creates an eight-digit code in the portal. Enter the school server address and those eight numbers in the app. The code works once and expires after ten minutes.

Is the website the same as the Android app?

The native Android app is for giving reading assessments. The school portal is for preparing sheets, managing classes and reviewing results. The short preview above only demonstrates the timer cue.

Can I correct a saved test?

Yes. Open Saved tests, choose the reading, and use Correct this test with your teacher PIN. The original remains protected; the correction is saved as a revision.
